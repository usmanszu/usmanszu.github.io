Research Objective

My research focuses on developing structured, geometry-aware, and data-efficient representation learning methods for 3D skeleton-based human action understanding. I integrate self-supervised contrastive learning, joint-aware knowledge transfer, geometric algebra, geometry-aware modeling, and complementary spectral and language-derived semantic information, with an emphasis on robust, efficient, and transferable human-motion representations.
