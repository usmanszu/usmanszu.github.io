## Research Objective

Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at Shenzhen University (expected November 2026), specializing in self-supervised 3D human-motion representation learning. My research combines contrastive learning, geometric algebra, graph-based models, spatiotemporal Transformers, and knowledge distillation. I seek a postdoctoral position to advance geometry-aware and efficient learning for human-motion understanding, multimodal perception, and intelligent systems.

## Research Interests

Self-Supervised and Contrastive Learning; **3D Skeleton-Based Action Recognition**; Geometric Algebra and Geometric Deep Learning; Graph Neural Networks and Spatiotemporal Transformers; Knowledge Distillation; Multimodal Human-Motion Understanding.
