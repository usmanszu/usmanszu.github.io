# Muhammad Usman

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Information and Communication Engineering at Shenzhen University, China (expected completion November 2026). I develop self-supervised methods for 3D skeleton-based human action recognition, with an emphasis on geometry-aware representation learning, efficient graph and Transformer models, contrastive learning, and knowledge distillation.

## Research interests

- Self-supervised and contrastive learning for 3D skeleton-based action recognition
- Geometric algebra, geometric deep learning and graph neural networks
- Spatiotemporal Transformers and human-motion understanding
- Knowledge distillation and efficient representation learning
- Multimodal perception and motion representations
